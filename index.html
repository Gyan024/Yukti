About Yukti Capital

India's Blended Finance
Execution Partner

Yukti — meaning "wisdom" or "strategy" in Sanskrit — reflects our approach: applying intelligent, structured thinking to unlock capital flows into India's most critical sectors at scale.

We are an impact advisory firm operating at the intersection of development finance and commercial capital. Unlike pure advisory firms, we bring execution capability — designing structures, aligning stakeholders, and seeing capital through to deployment.
