What We Do

End-to-end advisory and transaction execution — from structuring to capital close — for blended finance deals in India's high-impact sectors.

Our Services

We offer a focused suite of services built around one core insight: that the gap between development capital and commercial capital is not ideological — it is structural. Our work creates the architecture to bridge it.

01
Core Service
Blended Finance Structuring

We design financial structures that de-risk impact investments enough to attract commercial capital — without compromising the development mandate of anchor investors.

→First-loss tranche design and sizing — calibrated to mobilise maximum commercial capital per rupee of concessional capital deployed
→Credit guarantee architecture — designing guarantee mechanisms that are credible to commercial lenders and fiscally sustainable for guarantee providers
→Layered fund structures — senior, mezzanine, and junior tranche design for pooled vehicles with multiple investor types
→Risk-sharing agreement design between DFIs, foundations, and commercial co-investors
02
Transaction Execution
End-to-End Deal Management

We don't stop at the term sheet. We manage transactions from structure design through legal documentation, investor onboarding, regulatory compliance, and capital close.

→Term sheet drafting and negotiation between diverse investor types with conflicting mandates
→Legal structuring coordination — working with transaction counsel on fund documentation, security creation, and regulatory compliance
→Investor onboarding and due diligence support for all parties in a transaction
→Capital close project management — milestone tracking, condition precedent management, and disbursement coordination
03
Investor Advisory
Capital Deployment Strategy

For impact investors and DFIs entering or expanding in India, we provide strategic advice on deployment mandates, sector prioritisation, and portfolio structuring.

→India market entry strategy for international DFIs, foundations, and impact funds
→Investment mandate design — defining sector focus, instrument mix, risk appetite, and return expectations
→Pipeline identification and preliminary due diligence for deployment partners and co-investment opportunities
→Portfolio construction and blended finance vehicle design for foundations and family offices
04
Impact Architecture
Impact Measurement & Reporting

We build impact measurement frameworks that are rigorous, proportionate, and genuinely useful — not compliance theatre that satisfies reporting requirements but reveals nothing.

→Theory of change development aligned to SDGs, IRIS+, and investor-specific frameworks
→KPI design for deployment partners — measurable, attributable, and cost-effective to collect
→Impact reporting frameworks for fund managers and transaction SPVs
→Portfolio-level impact aggregation and investor-facing reporting templates
