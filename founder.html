About Hari

Two Decades at the Frontier of Impact Capital

Hari Rajagopal founded Yukti Capital with a clear conviction: that India's development financing challenge is not a shortage of intent, but a shortage of structured pathways to deploy it. His career has been built on creating those pathways — across institutions, instrument types, and impact sectors.

With over two decades of experience spanning development finance, corporate banking, and impact advisory, Hari brings a rare combination of financial depth and development domain expertise. He has worked across the full capital spectrum — from DFIs and multilaterals to commercial banks and impact funds — and understands the incentives, constraints, and mandates of every seat at the blended finance table.

Before founding Yukti Capital, Hari held senior roles at leading financial institutions where he built expertise in structured credit, project finance, and financial inclusion. His work included designing credit structures for underserved segments, building risk-sharing frameworks with development partners, and managing complex multi-stakeholder transactions from origination to close.

He brings to Yukti Capital not just technical expertise, but a genuine commitment to India's development challenge — a belief that building a fairer, more sustainable economy requires patient, structured, and expertly executed financial intervention at scale.
